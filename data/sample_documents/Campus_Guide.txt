BUDDY CAMPUS NAVIGATION & SERVICES

SECTION: Campus Layout & Building Directory
The campus is divided into three primary zones:
1. Academic Zone:
   - Main Academic Block (CSE, Civil Engineering, Dean Academics)
   - Science & Tech Annex (ECE, Physics, Chemistry Labs)
   - Engineering Workshop Block (Mechanical Labs, Carpentry, CNC Machines)
2. Administrative Zone:
   - Admin Block (Director Office, Registrar Room 102, Accounts Counter 3 & 4)
   - Convention Center (Dr. APJ Abdul Kalam Auditorium, Seminar Halls)
3. Residential & Sports Zone:
   - Aryabhatta & Ramanujan Boys Hostels
   - Gargi & Kalpana Girls Hostels
   - Indoor Badminton Courts, Olympic Swimming Pool, Cricket Ground

SECTION: Visitors, Parking & Security Instructions
Visitors Protocol:
- All visitors must register at Gate 1 (Main Entrance) and exchange a government photo ID for a Visitor Badge.
- Visitor vehicles must park in Parking Lot P-1 near the main security outpost.
- High-speed campus Wi-Fi guest vouchers can be acquired at the Reception Kiosk or Admin Room 102.
- Campus emergency medical line: Ext. 911 or direct phone: 011-23456789.
