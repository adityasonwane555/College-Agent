SECTION: Admission Eligibility Criteria
Undergraduate Programs (B.Tech):
1. Candidates must have secured a minimum of 60% aggregate marks in 10+2 examinations with Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics from a recognized central or state board.
2. Direct merit seats are allocated via State Joint Entrance Examination (Rank < 15,000) or National Level Entrance Test (JEE Main percentile >= 85).
3. Lateral entry for Diploma holders is available directly into the 2nd year (3rd semester) for qualifying students with >= 65% in their engineering diploma.

Postgraduate Programs (M.Tech / MBA):
1. M.Tech candidates must hold a valid B.E./B.Tech degree in a relevant discipline with a valid GATE score or Institute Entrance Test score.
2. MBA candidates must possess any Bachelor's degree (minimum 3 years) with 50% aggregate and valid CAT/MAT/State CET scores.

SECTION: Fee Structure & Scholarships
Tuition Fees:
- B.Tech Tuition Fee: Rs. 85,000 per semester.
- M.Tech Tuition Fee: Rs. 65,000 per semester.
- MBA Tuition Fee: Rs. 95,000 per semester.
- One-time caution deposit (Refundable): Rs. 10,000.

Scholarships & Financial Aid:
- Merit-Cum-Means Scholarship: 50% tuition fee waiver for top 5% rank holders whose annual family income is below Rs. 4,00,000.
- Sports Excellence Scholarship: 100% sports quota tuition waiver for national-level athletes.
- Women in STEM Fellowship: 25% waiver for all female candidates enrolling in Computer Science and Electronics.

SECTION: Campus Facilities & Anti-Ragging Policy
Campus Hostels:
Hostel allotment is managed online via ERP. Both AC and non-AC rooms are available on twin-sharing and triple-sharing basis. Mess provides vegetarian and non-vegetarian food certified by FSSAI.

Zero Tolerance Anti-Ragging Policy:
The institution adheres strictly to UGC Regulations on Curbing the Menace of Ragging in Higher Educational Institutions. Any student found engaging in ragging will face immediate suspension and legal reporting. Helpline: 1800-180-5522.
